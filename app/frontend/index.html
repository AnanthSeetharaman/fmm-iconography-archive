How Uncataloged Mudras, Asanas & Ayudhas Are Auto-Learned

When an author uploads an iconographic plate describing a gesture (like Shikhara Mudra), the system does not fail or require code modifications. It traverses a 7-stage autonomous learning pipeline:

1
Plate Ingestion
High-resolution plate uploaded and stored permanently under /storage/images/{slug}/.
Filesystem Storage
2
Windows Media OCR
Native Windows OCR extracts uncompressed token streams with bounding coordinates and confidence.
WinRT Layer 1
3
Sanskrit IAST Repair
Deterministic correction rules repair corrupted diacritics (e.g. GaQeSa → Gaṇeśa, ÄsTna → Āsīna).
IAST Normalizer
4
Shastra Pattern NER
Entity regex detects gestures (... mudrā, ... hasta), postures (... āsana) and mounts (... vāhana).
Autonomous Discovery
5
Curator Verification
Curator inspects extracted proposals, confidence scores, and preview of tables to be impacted in OCR Studio.
Human-In-The-Loop
6
Atomic DuckDB Upsert
Single transaction writes to taxonomy_terms, creates aliases in term_aliases, links study_taxonomy_mappings, and logs to slide_ocr_data.
ACID Multi-Table Commit
7
Instant Search Auto-Focus
Queries for "mudra", "hasta", or "shikhara" immediately re-rank the monograph and auto-focus on the exact plate.

About Five Metal Masonry

Nurturing & Preserving Panchaloha Bronzes.

Five Metal Masonry was born out of sheer love for South Indian Bronze Panchaloha sculptures. We began this journey in early 2024 with an aim to nurture and preserve this ancient art form, making sacred bronze icons accessible while honoring canonical tradition.

We believe our intricately crafted bronze icons transcend words — they speak to the soul, evoke devotional emotion, create lifetime memories, and carry timeless stories down through generations. Collaborating with both generational sthapatis and emerging master artisans, we bridge living heritage with computational iconography.

Iconography & Iconometry Deep study of Shilpa Shastra proportions (Talamana), attributes (Ayudha), and gestures (Mudra).
Authentic Panchaloha Traditional lost-wax (Madhuchehishtavidhana) casting using copper, zinc, lead, silver & gold.
Living Master Craftsmen Direct partnership with hereditary artisans from Swamimalai, Thanjavur, and Kumbakonam.
Independent Digital Archive Permanent, high-resolution study monographs beyond the transient feed of social media.
OCR Ingestion Automated Shastra term extraction, metadata auditing, and analytical indexing.
Social & Community Active study discourse on Instagram @five_metal_masonry & Facebook.

Sthapati Canon & Mathematical Iconometry

The Sacred Geometry of Panchaloha

The sacred knot and icosahedral lattice represent the geometric harmonic proportions codified in the Manasara and Kashyapa Shilpa Shastra. Every Panchaloha bronze is sculpted using the Talamana proportional grid:

• Dasatala (10 Units): Divine proportions of primary cosmic deities (Nataraja, Vishnu, Shiva).
• Navatala (9 Units): Canonical measure for secondary divinities and Ganapati.
• Panchaloha Alloy: Five-element metallurgical synthesis of Copper (Tamra), Zinc (Yasada), Lead (Sisaka), Silver (Rajata), and Gold (Suvarna).
• Madhuchehishtavidhana: The ancient, unbroken lost-wax casting technique perfected over a thousand years.
