## 🏛️ 1. Domain Philosophy & Living Heritage

### 1.1 The Panchaloha Sacred Tradition
Five Metal Masonry documents and preserves the living tradition of South Indian **Panchaloha** (Sanskrit: *Pañcaloha* - "Five Metals") sacred bronze casting:
- **Alloy Composition:** Traditionally composed of **Copper (*Tāmra*)** as the dominant base (~80–85%), alloyed with **Tin/Zinc (*Kāṃsya/Pitala*)**, **Lead (*Sīsa*)**, and ritually consecrated proportions of **Gold (*Svarṇa*)** and **Silver (*Rajatā*)**.
- **Sculptural Technique:** Practiced via **Madhuchchishtavidhana** (*Madhūcchiṣṭavidhāna* - Cire Perdue / Lost-Wax Casting), producing solid, one-of-a-kind monolithic bronze icons that are never hollow or machine-replicated.
- **Epigraphic Continuity:** Inherited directly from the Pallava (7th–9th c. CE), Imperial Chola (9th–13th c. CE), and Vijayanagara (14th–16th c. CE) sculptural gilds (*Sthapatis*).

### 1.2 Canonical Shilpa Shastra Authority
All iconographic analysis in the archive is validated against the foundational classical manuals (*Shilpa Shastras* and *Vaikhanasa/Pancharatra Agamas*):
1. **Kāśyapa Śilpa Śāstra (*Kashyapa Shilpa Shastra*)**: The pre-eminent text for Chola bronze proportions, bipedal stances, facial features, and divine weapons.
2. **Mayamata (*Mayamatam*)**: Comprehensive treatise on iconometry (*Tāla māna*), temple architecture, and pedestal (*Pīṭha*) design.
3. **Mānasāra (*Manasara*)**: Architectural and iconographic manual articulating divine measurement systems (*Daśatāla*, *Navatāla*).
4. **Śrītattvanidhi (*Shritattvanidhi*)**: 19th-century encyclopedic compilation codifying dhyana shlokas for the 32 forms of Ganesha and multifaceted deities.
5. **Mudgala Purāṇa & Gaṇeśa Purāṇa**: Primary theological treatises governing Ganapatya iconography.

## 🐘 3. Landmark Case Studies Codified

### 3.1 Gaṇeśa: Variations in Iconography (Study 001)
- **Canonical Scope:** Focuses on the visible, anatomical, and compositional variations expressed in South Indian bronze icons rather than merely theological epithets.
- **Anatomical Variations (Bāhu, Mukha, Śuṇḍā):**
  - **Arms (*Bāhu-bheda*):** *Dvibhuja* (2 arms), *Caturbhuja* (4 arms - standard), *Ṣaḍbhuja* (6 arms), *Aṣṭabhuja* (8 arms), *Daśabhuja* (10 arms), *Ṣoḍaśabhuja* (16 arms).
  - **Heads (*Mukha-bheda*):** *Ekamukha* (1 head), *Dvimukha* (2 heads), *Trimukha* (3 heads), *Pañcamukha* (5 heads - Heramba).
  - **Trunk Orientation (*Śuṇḍā-bheda*):**
    - *Vāmāvarta* (curving to the proper left towards the sweet Modaka - most common, domestic benevolence).
    - *Dakṣiṇāvarta* (curving to the proper right - solar, tantric, requiring rigorous Agamic puja).
    - *Triśuṇḍa* (three-trunked - rare iconographic form documented in Pune).
    - *Central / Descending* (trunk hanging straight down).
- **Associated Vāhana:** *Mūṣika* (IAST: *Mūṣika*, phonetic alias: *Mooshika*), symbolizing the subduing of ego and restless desires.

### 3.2 Naṭarāja: Ananda Tandava & Cosmic Equilibrium (Study 002)
- **Prabhāmaṇḍala:** Circular arch of flaming tongues symbolizing the cosmic cycle of time and manifestation.
- **Hand Gestures:**
  - Rear Right: Holds *Ḍamaru* (sound of cosmic creation, *Sṛṣṭi*).
  - Rear Left: Bears *Agni* in a crescent cup (fire of dissolution, *Saṃhāra*).
  - Front Right: Displays *Abhaya-mudrā* (divine protection, *Sthiti*).
  - Front Left: Sweeps across the chest in *Gajahasta* (elephant-trunk posture pointing down to the raised left foot, signifying *Tirobhāva* and *Anugraha* / salvation).
- **Prostrate Dwarf (*Apasmāra Puruṣa*):** Subdued beneath the right stamping foot (*Kūṭikāpāda*), personifying spiritual ignorance (*Avidyā*).

### Ingestion Standard Operating Rules:
1. **IAST Diacritic Standard:** All Sanskrit terms must be captured in canonical IAST (*International Alphabet of Sanskrit Transliteration*): e.g. `Gaṇeśa`, `Naṭarāja`, `Lalitāsana`, `Mūṣika`.
2. **Spelling Aliases Generation:** Every new canonical term must automatically register phonetic transliterations (e.g. `Mooshika`, `Mushika`, `Mooshikavahana`) into `term_aliases` to allow fuzzy scholar search.
3. **Plate & Slide Referencing:** Any discussion of visual traits must bind to exact 1-indexed slide numbers (`slide_numbers = [2, 3]`).
